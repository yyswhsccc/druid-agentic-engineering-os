Druid turns AI coding into an operating system

The strongest signal is not that Druid found bugs. The strongest signal is that Yongshan built the architecture that lets Druid keep finding, validating, maintaining, and learning from engineering work autonomously inside defined strategy and human-supervision boundaries.

Scanner

Discovers repositories, issues, bounties, open review surfaces, recent maintainer activity, collision risk, and reward/merge signals.

Decision engine

Ranks tasks by value, risk, testability, review friction, bounty fit, and maintenance cost. It can stop work when a PR becomes stale or low expected value.

Patch and test generator

Builds small patches with direct regression coverage. For money/security paths, it prefers PoC-to-fix over broad refactors.

Review loop

Tracks CI, maintainer comments, labels, requested changes, merge/close state, and whether a maintenance note is useful or just noise.

Adaptive memory

Learns from merged, closed, superseded, credited, dirty, and zombie PRs. Strategy changes become reusable operating rules.

Human gates

Designed for low touch after setup, not zero oversight. High-risk decisions, policy shifts, and final approvals remain human-in-the-loop.
